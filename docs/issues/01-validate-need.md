# D01 — Validate the local evidence-audit need and contribution path

Status: draft. Suggested labels: `research`, `product-validation`. Milestone: M0. Estimate: 4–8 focused hours plus participant availability. Dependencies: none.

## Problem

Scheduling complaints establish pain, not a market for another CLI. Historical summaries and monitors already exist. Validate the narrower workflow before investing in a full collector and release.

## Deliverable

A short, anonymized findings memo from five independent maintainers discussing real incidents, existing tooling, privacy constraints, willingness to review history assumptions and the practical value of a reproducible report. Include a contribution-versus-separate-project comparison for Cronkeeper, Schedule Lens and cron-drift. Outreach requires the maintainer's explicit authorization; this draft does not send messages.

## Acceptance criteria

- Separate direct requests, observed behavior, polite interest and author interpretation.
- Show the report's ambiguous/unknown cases, not just ideal results.
- Record whether the report changes a real decision or support request.
- Evaluate the proposed gate: three concrete unmet evidence tasks, two independent prototype uses, one repeat use before expansion; record failures as well as successes.
- End with continue, contribute or stop, with supporting evidence. If recruitment fails, mark the study incomplete rather than claiming validation.

## Out of scope

Growth campaigns, fabricated testimonials, surveys presented as representative market estimates, product implementation and public issue posting on other projects without authorization.
